Search Tips

  • Enter a surname to search for all matching records
  • Use first name or initials to narrow results
  • Service numbers are unique to soldiers
  • Decorations (DSO, MC, etc.) apply to officers only
  • Branch of Army filters by regiment or corps
  • Theatre of War groups locations (e.g. Western Front, Gallipoli)
  • Born / Residence Region filters by England, Scotland, Wales, Ireland, or Overseas
  • Query Options: AND = all criteria must match, OR = any can match, NOT = exclude matches
  • Search Mode: Basic shows all options in every dropdown; Advanced automatically narrows dropdowns based on your current filters
  • Leave fields blank to include all records

About This Database

This database contains 703,806 military personnel records from the First World War (1914-1919), including 41,846 officers and 661,960 soldiers.

SDGW 1914-1919

Preserving the history and records of those who served in the Great War 1914-1919. An historical resource for veterans' families and researchers.
